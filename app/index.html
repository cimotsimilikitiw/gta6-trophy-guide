Roadmap to platinum

  1. Play the story first. Complete the main chapters, finale, and named story missions. Story trophies form the natural backbone of the list and unlock systems you will need later.
  2. Clean up activities early. Work on races, shooting ranges, drone races, challenges, and combat trophies while you travel. Many can be combined with normal exploration.
  3. Track collectibles and locations. Exploration trophies like 100% completion, vehicles, WAiNK locations, meditation spots, and properties are easiest when you keep a running checklist.
  4. Do missable-feeling tasks carefully. Most trophies can be attempted in free roam, but read each guide before major story or heist moments and keep a manual save when possible.
  5. Finish the platinum mop-up. After story and major activities, compare your earned count against the 55-trophy total and clear remaining shop, robbery, property, and challenge trophies.

Notes are based on official trophy names/descriptions and public trophy-listing sources. This guide is original text and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or PlayStation.
