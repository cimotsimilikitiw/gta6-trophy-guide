Roadmap

A suggested order; play your way.

  1. 1. Play the story naturally

    Finish the main chapters and named story missions first. Keep the spoiler toggle on if you want the checklist to stay discreet during a first playthrough.

  2. 2. Clean up activities and strangers

    After the credits, mop up stranger strands, races, shooting ranges, drone races, and other open-world activities. These stack well with exploration.

  3. 3. Sweep collectibles and properties

    Track meditation spots, WAiNK locations, vehicles, card sets, and garage properties. Use the kind filter to focus on collectibles.

  4. 4. Finish combat and free-roam misc

    Handle any remaining wanted-level, robbery, combat, or oddball trophies. Save 100% completion and the platinum for last.
