Platinum roadmap

A practical order for clearing all 55 trophies.

  1. Play the story naturally. Complete chapter and mission trophies as they appear. Story trophies are marked in the list; use the spoiler toggle if you want descriptions hidden.
  2. Clean up activities early. Work on races, shooting ranges, stunts, and repeatable challenges while exploring so they do not pile up at the end.
  3. Track collectibles and exploration. Meditation spots, WAiNK It locations, vehicle discoveries, properties, and card sets are easiest when you keep a running checklist.
  4. Finish combat and money tasks. Bank vaults, wanted-level escapes, laundering, and spending goals can be planned around your free-roam route.
  5. Final sweep for platinum. After 100% completion and all misc trophies, "Vice Got a Price!" should unlock as the final platinum.

Missable flags: No trophies are marked missable based on the official trophy descriptions alone. If post-launch information changes this, verify against current patch notes and trusted trackers.
